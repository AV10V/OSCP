principals in BloodHound and in `whoami /groups`, the GUID for anything that addresses the domain by
identifier rather than name.

## 7.2 What this chapter will document

1. **The path in** — the ACL edge, delegation, ADCS template, or credential from MS02 that grants
   domain control, and the BloodHound query that found it.
2. **The escalation** — the exact commands, with the output that proves the boundary was crossed
   (a DCSync of `krbtgt`, a DA shell, or an ADCS certificate issued to a controllable principal).
3. **The flag** — `proof.txt` read as Domain Admin, with `whoami`, `hostname`, and `ipconfig` in the
   same session so the capture is attributable.
