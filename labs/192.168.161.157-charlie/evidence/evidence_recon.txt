Notes
- nmap labels 20000 as "Webmin httpd". Webmin's default port is 10000; 20000 is Usermin.

  paths, and the version 1.820 matches EDB 50234 (Usermin <= 1.820), not CVE-2019-15107
  (Webmin 1.890-1.920).
- 21/tcp is the only service that volunteers data before authentication.
